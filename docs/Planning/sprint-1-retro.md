DevFlow Sprint 1 Retrospective
What Went Well

The Sprint 1 backlog became clearer after converting rough backlog phrases into complete user stories. Adding acceptance criteria also made it easier to understand what would need to be tested before considering each story complete.

Using story points helped identify that password reset and session security require more consideration than the original short backlog phrases suggested.

The Asana board also provided a clearer view of the sprint workflow and dependencies.

What Did Not Go Well

The original backlog items were too brief and did not provide enough information for someone else to immediately understand the expected behaviour.

The initial Sprint 1 scope also contained fewer stories than required for the capstone exercise, which required the authentication scope to be expanded and refined.

Action for Sprint 2

Before starting Sprint 2, I will ensure every selected backlog item has a clear user story, acceptance criteria, an estimate, and a defined reason for being included in the sprint.

I will also review the sprint scope before execution to make sure the selected work is realistic and sufficiently detailed.
